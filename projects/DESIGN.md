# santdev / Black Editorial Portfolio

## Purpose
A recruiter-facing AI and tech portfolio connecting documented IT operations experience with automation, NivIA QA, cloud and 15 public software projects. Current eSIM's experience is first: AI Specialist (self-employed, remote), May 2026 to present, supplied by the user from their LinkedIn position.

## Reference and craft
Atrium https://atrium-template.modulify.website/ inspected with Chromium/Playwright DevTools. Reference observations: one dominant centered title, compact navigation, vertical scrolling narrative, restrained image/text rhythm. We use no original template assets, code, photography or typography.

## Story architecture
- Opening viewport: centered lowercase santdev. wordmark, black background, tiny blue accent, scroll cue.
- Introduction: broad typographic thesis about operations and AI.
- Selected work: NivIA as a QA engineering study, Oliv-e Sa?de as documented automation, Abyssal as local private prototype.
- Career: chronological five-employer ordered list from current eSIM's back to Tech Mahindra, revealed by ordinary vertical scroll.
- Projects: 15 public software projects in responsive grid with category filters, search and real demo/source links.
- About and contact: capabilities, certifications, education and professional contact.

## Design system
Background #080808, surface #101112, raised #17191b, ivory #f3f1eb, muted #b9b9bb and dusty blue #9eafff.
Barlow Condensed for title/headlines, Instrument Sans for body and DM Mono for metadata.
Max container width 1480px, gutters clamp(20px,4.2vw,66px).
Purposeful blue only for dot, focus, current role and scroll progress. No video, fake dashboards, heavy glass, decorative stock media or scroll hijacking.

## Testing contract
Skills: local frontend-design, ECC design-system, frontend-design-direction, browser-qa and frontend-a11y.
Node tests; Playwright browsers at 1440/768/390/320; axe automated WCAG 2/2.1 A/AA; no horizontal overflow or console errors.
Reduced motion respected. All case-study facts qualified, NivIA QA not falsely presented as production.
